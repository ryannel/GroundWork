# Bound the non-map plan pages

Budget 02 measured all eight Today/graph pages at 1,444–1,493 KB, Sourced Financing Terms and Immutable IDs team pages at 2,289 and 1,068 KB, and Care SE programme at 1,201 KB against the existing 1,024 KB limit.

The read-only source measurement used the canonical Volvo runtime-03 copy. The graph holds 27,329 JSON bytes, including 25,879 bytes in sixteen subgraphs. Today/graph had rendered those nested subgraphs and each subgraph again as a separate full record. It now renders each recorded graph and subgraph name and authored summary, with an exact selected-version thing link. The full graph, its nested fields and cited source records remain available on that record page.

The Sourced Financing Terms team page repeated 54,039 bytes of assigned change records and 44,114 bytes of milestone, decision, question and risk metadata. Immutable IDs repeated 11,573 and 46,500 bytes respectively. Team pages now keep their work, issue state, dates, dependency and update summaries and link to the full work records. All assigned changes, decisions, questions and risks have labelled links to their actual change screen or thing record. Risk and question labels retain the authored statement or question.

Care SE contains five plans with 37 work items and ten milestones. The programme now lists all plans and renders the selected plan's work, milestones and independent updates. The default is the first plan in the existing declared build order. Links use the actual plan folder in the query. Partner dependencies remain listed with exact work item links. The independent partner page retains its existing selection.

Twenty-one focused tests passed across native plan rendering and workspace routes. They prove that a nested graph composition remains on its exact before record, team detail links open the full requirement, and selecting the second programme plan preserves its full work body while the other plan stays linked. Scoped lint, incremental TypeScript and whitespace checks pass. Production build and page budget are owned by the final gate.
