# Customer service question review

The final result is **5 supported, 16 partial and 1 unsupported**, against the same 22 questions and
required-evidence clauses fixed in [the original review](../customer-service-pipeline/answerability-review.md).
Q05 and Q14 move from partial to supported. Q01, Q07 and Q08 retain their supported result; no other
question is promoted. A passing extractor or validator does not establish question closure.

The only repository is `volvo-cars/cds-ecom-customer-service`, pinned at
`09623ab3a1f39845d8fc02abc3d591b53a3b5fea`. The reviewed canonical document is
`reports/customer-service-bindings-001/final/document.json`, backed by the final source capture
`reports/customer-service-bindings-002/repository.json`. Other repository scans remain stopped.
The source contracts do not establish deployed behaviour, external wire serialisation, durable writes,
remote freshness or effective external framework configuration.

The independent [source review](source-review.md) fixes the Q05 path and the complete Q14 operation
inventory before the binding result is accepted. The final bounded review selected and retrieved all
**5,669 records in 1,418 pages (3,015,569 bytes)**. Each request selects at most 32 IDs; each page returns
at most four records and 65,536 bytes. The complete selection is capped at 8,192 records and 16 MiB.
Proofs receive a document rebuilt exclusively from returned records, with unrelated collections empty.
The snapshot is `6b9e671750d58b91750b6356efce8751f3452ef7771dde0c39e07d8e690247b3`.

The final receipt passes the 19-route Q01 inventory, all six Q05 clauses, all five Q14 clauses and the
selected eight-clause Fleet source path. All **56 mutations reject**: 13 for Q05, 29 for Q14 and 14 for
the Fleet boundary. They delete or corrupt typed relations and bindings while preserving node display
text. Mutation rejection is counted only after the corresponding unmodified baseline passes. This
caught and corrected a real proof hole: disconnected catch nodes initially sufficed; the final proof
requires the request's owning try to reach each catch through a typed relation.

An independent audit verifies all **2,555 returned source anchors across 253 files** against the pinned
Git tree, blob hashes and line bounds, with lazy fetching disabled. Final binding pages are identical
to those source-audited pages. Seven narrower native proofs and fourteen declaration probes were
rerun through the bounded API; all 21 evidence sets retain identical IDs and values in the final source
capture. Q08's default-value proof was independently rerun solely from returned pages, with eight
additional relation-removal mutations rejected. Neither older execution proofs nor raw-source reading
substitute for a missing captured relation.
