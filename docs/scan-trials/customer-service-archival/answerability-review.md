# Customer archival answerability review

Thirteen of the twenty-two frozen questions are supported, eight are partial and one is unsupported. Q19 and Q20 are the only status changes from the [preceding accepted review](../customer-service-messaging/answerability-review.md). Every original question and required evidence clause is retained in the [machine-readable review](answerability.json); earlier supported questions are replayed.

The sole repository is `volvo-cars/cds-ecom-customer-service` at `09623ab3a1f39845d8fc02abc3d591b53a3b5fea`. Other repository scans remain stopped. The [independent source oracle](source-review.md) fixes the Q19/Q20 source criteria. The [original denominator](../customer-service-pipeline/answerability-review.md) remains unchanged.

The accepted source capture is `reports/customer-service-archival-002/repository.json`; the canonical enrichment is `reports/customer-service-archival-001/accepted/document.json`. The combined bounded receipt is `reports/customer-service-archival-001/accepted-review-final/receipt.json`. It retrieves all 45,616 selected records in 11,409 pages (21,241,684 serialised bytes), within explicit caps of 49,152 records and 32 MiB. Requests select at most thirty-two IDs; pages return at most four records and 64 KiB. Oversized records are rejected. Source verification covers 10,987 anchors in 260 files at the pinned local Git revision. These checks establish neither remote nor production currency.

Q19 passes twenty-nine clauses covering exact filters, CustomerUpdate precedence, mapped-reason/event lineage, the active save and restore(false) branches, scoped catches and save-before-delete order. Q20 passes twelve clauses covering source schedule/condition/resource declarations, lock/provider/async/executor joins, exact iteration/helper applications, catches and typed integer counter increments. All 225 combined mutations reject: the prior 182 plus twenty-one Q19 and twenty-two Q20 mutations. New mutation rejection requires an applied change and a fully passing whole-question baseline. The Q20 result is unchanged when the bounded pages are reversed.

The declarations retain runtime uncertainty. Named executor core/max pool one and SchedulerLock do not establish global full-job mutual exclusion: the scheduled invocation dispatches a void async job and ignores completion. Per-customer failures normally allow the next iteration, while lookup/iteration or catch-path failures can end the job. Archive upsert, active delete and counters do not establish atomic rollback or durable success; a failure after the success increment can also increment failure.

Fresh native replay preserves the seven original probes and eight Q08 default mutations. The original strict replay is retained at `reports/customer-service-archival-001/independent-native/accepted-native-replay.json`: its Q09 identity comparison fails after the parser exposes a previously missing catch-type relation elsewhere in that file. The explicit opt-in replay at `reports/customer-service-archival-001/independent-native-migrated/accepted-native-replay.json` verifies all seven probes semantically. Six retain exact record identity. For Q09, a unique bijection maps 200 semantic-edge IDs; every other edge field and all 176 non-edge IDs and payloads remain exact. Fresh bounded requests use the mapped current IDs before the unchanged proofs run. The default strict identity gate remains unchanged. The [migration audit](../../../reports/customer-service-archival-001/independent-migration-audit.json) passes seventeen adversarial cases; changed endpoints, relation, source, owner, position or non-edge records reject. This is an identifier migration, with no relaxation of semantic facts. It does not claim that all old IDs are identical.

Q07/Q08 remain supported on their frozen local scope. Q09 remains partial despite its passing local probe and the Q19 restore(false) path. Q21 remains unsupported, and Q22 remains partial because remote/deployed state is unobserved.
