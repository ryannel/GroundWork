# Slice 6 — Two-direction traceability and the invalidation signal

Backward: every proof names the design anchor it comes from, and the row fails on an anchor that does not resolve.

Forward: every facing item the design names is claimed by exactly one slice's proof, or recorded as a deferral. Unclaimed is red, and claimed twice is red.

The forward half exists because of a recorded failure: a sealed pattern that belonged to no slice, where every slice was individually correct. Only this direction sees that.

Beside it, the invalidation signal: a bet declares the premises it stands on, and amending or withdrawing an artifact marks every later bet that cites it.
