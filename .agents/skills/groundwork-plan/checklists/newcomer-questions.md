# Newcomer questions every plan must answer

A newcomer reads the story in order and asks these questions. If the answer is not in the YAML, the page they
are on is empty or misleading. Use this list twice: while writing the plan (ask the room) and in review (check
the files).

**Where** is the YAML that holds the answer. Chapters 2 and 3 describe today, so their answers live in the
system model (`<workspace>/domains/<d>/systems/<s>/{system,contracts,handlers}.yaml`, `flows/`, `teams.yaml`),
not in the plan; a gap there is filled by a scan, not by copying into the plan. Chapters 1 and 4 to 8 live in
`plans/<plan>/plan.yaml`. **Check** is how to verify it by tool or by eye.

## Chapter 1 · Why

| #   | Question a newcomer asks                                                                        | Where                                       | Check                                                                                                                     |
| --- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1   | What ships, for whom, and why now?                                                              | `problem.summary`                           | 2–4 sentences, names the user and the capability, no jargon without a glossary entry                                      |
| 2   | What's the evidence? What happens today instead?                                                | `problem.why`                               | contains at least one number or quote                                                                                     |
| 3   | How will we know it worked?                                                                     | `problem.metrics[]` `{name, today, target}` | every metric has both `today` and `target`                                                                                |
| 4   | What are we _not_ doing?                                                                        | `problem.non_goals[]`                       | ≥ 2 entries; includes the obvious scope-creep ask                                                                         |
| 5   | How much time is this worth?                                                                    | `meta.appetite`                             | present; matches milestone count                                                                                          |
| 6   | Can I picture a real case?                                                                      | `problem.scenarios[]` with `flows`          | ≥ 2 scenarios; a named person and a concrete date/amount; at least one tags a flow of today and one an added flow ("new") |
| 7   | What does BFF / outbox / projection / PSP / dual-publish / Pact / ROAM / walking skeleton mean? | `problem.glossary[]`                        | every acronym or jargon term used in any string in the plan has an entry                                                  |

## Chapter 2 · The system today

| #   | Question                                                                                    | Where                                                                                          | Check                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| --- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 8   | Which teams are involved and who owns which component?                                      | plan `teams[]`; component `team` in the model                                                  | every team that owns a work item, test, risk or question exists in `teams` (Product too)                                                                                                                                              |
| 8a  | What is each system, component and store for?                                               | `does` in `system.yaml` and `contracts.yaml`                                                   | every one has a plain-words role (≤ 10 words, no code names); curated `does` in `system.yaml` wins over the scan's                                                                                                                    |
| 8b  | Which domain is this in, and who builds on it?                                              | `domains/<d>/` and its systems                                                                 | each system sits in the domain whose people own it; a shared host is split into one component per owned subgraph or module                                                                                                            |
| 8c  | Which of these are really one thing?                                                        | `system.yaml` `repos` and its components                                                       | deployables built and run as one (shared repo, a store only they share, sole caller) are components of one system; no component without a line, no topic without a message                                                            |
| 9   | Are the BFF, cron jobs, schedulers and third parties on the map?                            | component `kind` = `ui` / `job` / `external`                                                   | anything that starts a flow or is called by one is a component                                                                                                                                                                        |
| 10  | Who talks to whom, sync or async?                                                           | `flows/*.yaml` `steps[].via` (`api:*` = sync, `msg:*` = async); handler `call`/`publish` steps | every endpoint and message used today appears in a flow step or a handler; `npm run map -- callers <id>`                                                                                                                              |
| 11  | Where does the data live — which database, which table?                                     | `stores[]`, `schemas[].store`                                                                  | every `kind: record` has a `store`; stores have `tech` (Postgres, Redis, BigQuery …)                                                                                                                                                  |
| 12  | Who reads or writes each store besides its owner?                                           | `stores[].access[]`, handler `read`/`write` steps                                              | the components × stores grid has no surprise cells; consumers keep their own projections                                                                                                                                              |
| 13  | Which broker is the event on, and who owns the topic?                                       | `stores[]` with `kind: topic`, `owner`, `tech`; `messages[].topic`                             | every message has a topic store; the topic's `access` lists its consumers                                                                                                                                                             |
| 14  | What does each API accept and return; is it authenticated; safe to retry; how does it fail? | `interfaces[].endpoints[]` `request`, `response`, `auth`, `idempotent`, `errors[]`             | every endpoint on the change path has all five                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| 15  | Where do DTOs and payloads live?                                                            | `schemas[]` `kind: dto` / `payload`, referenced by `request`/`response`/`payload`              | no endpoint or message without its schema                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| 15b | What does the person actually see today, and where on the page is the thing we change?      | `screens[]` (`image`, `regions[].at`, `regions[].shows`), step `screen`                        | every page, email or print the feature touches has a screen with today's screenshot and the regions the change names; the steps that show it carry `screen:`; shows in 2.x _What people see today_ and the journey strip in chapter 3 |
| 15c | What will it look like after, and can I click through it?                                   | plan ops on `scr:` ids (`image`, `figma`, `mockup`, regions)                                   | every changed screen has a new picture or Figma link; a journey across 3+ screens has a `mockup` ([groundwork-mockup](../../groundwork-mockup/SKILL.md))                                                                              |
| 15a | Where is the code for this? Can I check that claim?                                         | `sources[]` `{repo, path, lines, commit, symbol?}` on every extracted fact                     | `node tools/sources.ts <workspace> --missing` reports none on the facts the plan touches                                                                                                                                              |

## Chapter 3 · How it runs today

| #   | Question                                                                      | Where                                                                                                   | Check                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| --- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 16  | What happens today, step by step, on the paths this change touches?           | `flows/*.yaml`; `handlers.yaml`                                                                         | every id a change modifies is used by a flow or a handler; the plan's ops target ids that exist                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| 17  | What does each component actually do at each step?                            | flow `steps[].logic`; the handler's step tree                                                           | present tense, today only; every business decision in the handler is an `if` with its condition in plain words                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| 18  | What goes wrong at each hop?                                                  | flow `steps[].fails`; handler `fail`, `try`/`catch`, `retry`                                            | present on every external call, publish and sync call                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| 19  | Which store does each step touch?                                             | flow `steps[].reads` / `writes`; handler `read` / `write`                                               | steps that persist anything name the record                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| 20  | Which steps will change?                                                      | derived from ops (◇ marker)                                                                             | nothing to author; if a step you expect isn't marked, the op is missing                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| 20a | Where does the person act, what systems respond, and when does the UI change? | `steps[].screen`, `action`, `from`/`to`, `via`, `logic`, `reads`/`writes`, `fails`; `screens[].regions` | Walk the starting interaction, backend work and visible result separately. Add to cart begins on the product page. Keep origin context through backend selection, including expanded mode; show the cart only at its update. Do not infer clicks, causality or delivery solely from screen links or list order. |

| #   | Question                                                     | Where                                          | Check                                                                                  |
| --- | ------------------------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 21  | What is the new journey end to end?                          | `changes[].ops[]` `add: flow`, `add: handler`  | ≥ 1 added flow, or the plan explains in `story.chapters.new` why nothing new is walked |

## Chapter 5 · What changes in what exists

| #   | Question                                  | Where                                                                  | Check                                                                                       |
| --- | ----------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 24  | Is this a new step or a changed contract? | `add: step` vs `modify: flow:x#step` / `modify: api:*` / `modify: h:*` | never modelled as a new copy of an existing thing; today is never copied into the plan      |
| 25  | What did it look like before, and after?  | `modify` ops with `set`/`append`/`drop`                                | before/after is derived; author only the after-value                                        |

| 27  | How is the old version retired?           | `add` with `supersedes` + `deprecate … replaced_by` (+ later `remove`) | every versioned message/endpoint has both halves; consumers drop off the old one per change |
| 28  | Migration, backfill, flag, rollback?      | `changes[].rollout`                                                    | present on every change that touches a store, a message, or a UI                            |
