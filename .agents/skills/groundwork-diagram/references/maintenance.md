# Maintain charts after new evidence

Use this workflow when a scan adds or changes evidence, a system joins an area, or a reviewed chart has
become hard to explain. The input is the changed evidence and existing diagrams, not a blank canvas.
Ordinary visual corrections need not restart a scan or re-audit unrelated repositories.

## Find what the change affects

Compare the previous and current retained records: identities and aliases, component membership,
contracts, invoked calls, event producers/consumers, resource users/owners, source revisions and coverage.
Record the input digest and source scopes actually reviewed. Changed labels or a fresh timestamp do not
establish new behaviour. An absent record in a partial scan does not justify deleting a known purpose.

Follow affected exchanges across system and area boundaries, including both directions and every user
of a shared resource. A system can contribute different work in several areas. Revisit those contexts,
not just the area containing the changed file. Reuse unchanged reviewed decisions and source receipts;
recheck them when their IDs, ownership, evidence, coverage or meaning changes.

## Reconcile facts before emphasis

Repair supported typed facts in their source model, preserving stable references and evidence strength.
Then reconsider the chart's interpretation:

- Does the new evidence change what is created, transformed, stored or consumed, or who benefits?
- Has independent work been mistaken for a single sequence, or a genuine collaboration been hidden?
- Does a new user make a formerly private store shared? Is a topic's publisher/owner now known?
- Do area-wide `columns` still place sources and consumers sensibly? Local component columns cannot
  decide the reading order of whole systems. Prefer a core anchor relevant to the selected overview.
- Does `overview` still explain the main boundary exchanges without secondary lookups or platform
  dependencies becoming spurious bridges? An explicitly independent process needs no invented line.
- Does a rolled-up label cover the actual reads, writes, events and material conditions? Keep secondary
  calls available; do not merely rename every exchange or choose a fixed number of arrows.

Use the production formats in [representation](representation.md). A new core exchange needs actual
support, not a desire for connectivity. Keep unsupported handoffs as bounded questions and investigate
new detached cards or groups with [the orphan audit](orphans.md).

## Check the changed experience

Validate affected model references, declared columns and narrative freshness. Refresh a system narrative's
basis only after reviewing its changed inputs; area flows require a separately recorded reviewed-input
ledger because they have no automatic per-system fingerprint. The read-only audit script is triage,
not approval of facts, directions or visual quality.

Inspect affected overviews and expanded systems at normal reading size, plus neighbouring boundaries.
The initial expanded viewport should lead with the system's responsibility in this area: another band's
late component column can bury it even when membership and geometric clearance are correct. A connection
label needs room on its own route; a generic character limit does not prove association in a short gutter.
A Fit map miniature proves footprint/orientation only. Check ordinary opening after a distant manual
fit, then confirm subsequent manual panning/zooming remains under reader control. Exercise representative
API, store and topic cutaways, including their senders and receivers. Compare default, selection, flow
filter and All connections without moving cards or losing identities. Review other areas affected by a
shared renderer change; mechanical box clearance alone does not prove route or text clarity.

Retain before/after captures and an area/system ledger with applied changes, orphan dispositions,
validation and review results. Carry unresolved evidence questions forward separately from rendering
failures. Finish the authorised local repairs and affected rechecks; do not stop at the first draft or
require a fresh approval merely because a reference describes a review step.
